# Academic office guide

DOC-29 · Version 0.1 · proposed · Owner: Registrar

**Purpose and scope** — Publish curricula and policy editions and administer scoped exceptions and quotas.

**Audience** — Registrar and delegated academic staff.

**Dependencies** — [Product roles and permissions](04-user-roles-permissions.md), [Academic domain and curriculum](05-academic-domain-curriculum.md), [Academic rule evaluation](06-academic-rule-engine.md), [Enrollment and waitlists](09-enrollment-waitlist.md).

## Create an academic edition
In the proposed academic-office workspace, select an authorized unit and term. Clone the applicable curriculum into a draft. Check degree level, specialization, requirement groups, course classifications, equivalencies and double-counting rules. Compare affected cohorts and produce sample degree audits. Create a policy edition with explicit thresholds and scoped exceptions. An independent academic approver evaluates rejected, accepted and exception examples before activation.
Assign editions at admission or through an approved transition. Compare existing and proposed outcomes before a student’s edition changes. Save effective date, actor, subject, case and reason. Check that unaffected students still resolve to the previous version.

## Quotas, exceptions and corrections
For a quota, inspect the physical pool, cross-listed sections and held offers. Enter an eligible group, seats within the total and release instant. Test before, at and after release. A reduction below live claims needs a remediation case. Never increase a physical total to resolve a policy exception.
For an exception, open the student case and select the exact rule, policy, term, expiry and permitted effect. Submit as requester. A different authorized approver checks scope and evidence. Re-evaluate the registration through the standard command and retain the receipt. For a corrected grade, append a new record revision, inspect dependent registrations, notify student and advisor and open review cases. Removal requires an independently authorized academic decision under the applicable policy.

## Decision and open question
Proposed: Require a different person for policy and exception sign-off. Assumed: Authorized staff can supply evidence for each exception or revision.

Needs approval (RISK-010) — Data stewardship and maker/checker delegations. Security lead: Test cross-scope grants and independent approval.
