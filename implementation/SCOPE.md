# Scope map

The implementation prompt authorizes the documented core. The documents classify most behaviors as proposed and provide no signed institutional policy. Development workflows may use explicitly fictional parameters. Operational activation depends on the approval owners listed in the specification. [Requirements catalog](../docx/artifacts/requirements.csv) · [Risk and decision register](../docx/artifacts/risks-assumptions-decisions.csv)

| Classification | Scope |
|---|---|
| Required for current release | Identity and scope, academic setup, calendars, records, enrollment and receipts, waitlists and exceptions, versioned scheduling, student planning, reports, imports and operational verification. Implementation status for each requirement appears in the coverage matrix. |
| Blocked by decision | Institutional queue and allocation inputs, final optimization and repair policy, privacy and retention, operational targets, production credentials and externally validated identity. |
| Proposed and awaiting scope approval | Full grading and transcript production, examination management, attendance, tuition and compensation, learning-platform synchronization and demand forecasting. |
| Optional | Additional optimizers, external projections and caching of public catalog metadata beyond the implemented private read paths. |

The implemented grade-history and curriculum APIs supply enrollment and degree-audit inputs. Transcript production and grade appeals remain extension work. [Extension boundaries](../docx/source/31-extension-modules.md)
